### What is Active on the Live Website:

1. **Brand Identity & Header**
   * Crimson Red & Dark Slate modern sports palette reflecting Nepal's energy.
   * Nepali script logo **KataTira (कततिर)** with live Bikram Sambat (B.S.) calendar date synchronization.
   * Top-level buttons for **"Host Tournament"**, **"My Passes"**, and **"Organizer Hub"**.

2. **Smart Search & Nepali Regional Filtering**
   * Multi-province dropdown covering all 7 provinces of Nepal (*Bagmati, Gandaki, Koshi, Lumbini, Madhesh, Karnali, Sudurpashchim*).
   * **"Near Me"** 1-tap local discovery toggle.
   * Date range filtering (*This Weekend, Next 7 Days, Bikram Sambat Month*).

3. **High-Impact Tournament & Event Cards**
   * Formatted prize pools in authentic Nepali currency style (e.g., `रू १,५०,०००` / `रू 2,00,000`).
   * **Verified Host Trust Badges** (Government Desk, Sports Association, Verified Club).
   * Key details: venue with Kathmandu/Pokhara/Lalitpur landmarks, dual English & B.S. dates, live team registration counter progress bars.
   * **1-Tap WhatsApp Share** and **Add to Calendar (Google & iCal)** on each card.

4. **Interactive Registration & Organizer Preview**
   * Instant registration modal with **eSewa & Khalti** payment simulation.
   * Scannable digital ticket pass with offline download.
   * Organizer onboarding with **Live Card Preview & Quality Checklist** before publishing.
